Eigenvector geometry

Reading the sphere

The three orange great-circle arcs join the population direction B, its estimate H, and the reference direction z. On the unit sphere, the length of each arc equals the angle between its endpoints, measured in radians.

The dotted circle passes through B and contains all directions making the same angle with z as B does. The dark geodesic from B meets the great circle through H and z at a right angle at H∗.

The coordinates are illustrative.
