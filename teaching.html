Student feedback

After the first midterm in my Fall 2026 statistics course, 40 of the 80 students responded to a survey. They asked for more opportunities to practice.

In end-of-term evaluations, students have singled out fair assessments, enthusiasm for the subject, and help during office hours. They have also asked for clearer lecture takeaways, closer connections between examples and assignments, and more timely feedback. I’m working on these questions with colleagues through UCLA’s 2026–27 CEILS PS-FIT fellowship.
