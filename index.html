Hubeyb Gurdogan.

I am a lecturer in the Department of Mathematics at UCLA. Previously, I was a Hedrick Assistant Adjunct Professor at UCLA from July 2023 to July 2026 and a postdoctoral scholar at UC Berkeley from December 2021 to July 2023. At Berkeley, I led a research collaboration with Swiss Re Institute on how a disruption at one supplier can cause losses across an entire supply chain. I received my Ph.D. in Financial Mathematics from Florida State University in 2021.

My research is in high-dimensional statistics, optimization, and mathematical finance. I study how errors in covariance estimates affect optimized portfolios and how to detect changes in the factors driving many time series from a short history of observations. More recently, I’ve become interested in identifying which series lead and which follow with a delay.
